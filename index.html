How it works

Get your audience engaged in under a minute.

1
Create a Session
Click "Start a Session" and get your unique 6-character room code instantly.
2
Build Your Polls
Add polls, Q&A, or word cloud activities to your session from the presenter dashboard.
3
Share the Link
Copy the auto-generated join link from your dashboard and paste it in the Google Meet chat. One click to join!
4
Go Live!
Activate a poll — your audience votes and you see results update in real time.

Already have a code?

Enter the room code shared by your presenter to join the session.
